# Selected development decisions

This is a curated decision guide, not a complete LLM transcript. [Selected conversation excerpts](SELECTED-TRACES.md) are included. The complete development conversation is not published.

- Directed: defined an everyday audience with basic market knowledge and a familiar conversation flow.
- Pushed back: rejected disconnected narration, synthetic delivery, repeated footage, and video that repeated the chat.
- Reviewed: identified lip-sync drift and looping cutaways; shortened presenter screen time and adjusted transitions.
- Trusted: delegated code-rendered graphics and assembly while reviewing the resulting videos.

Only the selected project exchanges are published. Credentials, provider receipts, unrelated conversations and private user chat histories are excluded. Product generation prompts are linked in PROMPTS.md.
